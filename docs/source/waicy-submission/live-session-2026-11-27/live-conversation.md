## Turn 1 · New caregiver

### User

Fictional scenario: An older adult said, “Later, I feel cold.” They had not bathed while I was there. What could I say, and which facts should I check?

### CareBridge

**What I understand**

They said, “Later, I feel cold,” and had not bathed while you were there. It’s not yet clear whether they agreed to bathe later.

**What you can try**

- Ask whether they still feel cold and what would help them feel comfortable.
- Check with the caregiver what was offered or tried, and whether bathing happened after you left.
- Respect their choice to wait or skip the bath today.

**Something you can say**

> I hear you feel cold. Would you like to wait, or skip the bath today?

我聽到你覺得冷。你想先等等，還是今天不洗澡？

</details>

**Limits**

- This is communication support, not professional care. The translation has not been reviewed by a native speaker.
