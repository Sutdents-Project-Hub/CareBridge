# Final-assistant conversation recording kit

The WAICY guidebook asks for at least ten exchanged messages between the entrant and the final edition of the assistant. A team member can record ten complete question–answer turns (20 messages) from the script below. The script uses the same fictional situation as the existing [agent-operated engineering replay](source/waicy-submission/live-session-2026-11-27/live-conversation.md). Use the actual new replies, including any defects. [Official guidebook, PDF page 21](https://waicy-cdn.wholeren.cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/WAICY-2026-Guidebook-2.pdf#page=21)

## Run a single session

Open the [live chatbot](https://carebridge-neon.vercel.app) in a fresh tab. Keep English selected. Use Settings to choose **New caregiver** and **Brief** for turns 1–6, then switch roles in the same tab as indicated. Click Send once per turn and wait for the reply. If an error occurs, record what happened and retry without changing reported facts. Keep any questions and replies verbatim.

| Turn | Role | Fictional message |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | New caregiver | Fictional scenario: An older adult said, “Later, I feel cold.” They had not bathed while I was there. What could I say, and which facts should I check? |
| 2 | New caregiver | They said, “I feel cold,” again. What can I say if I want to give them time to choose without pressuring them? |
| 3 | New caregiver | I offered a blanket. I left before I could see whether they took it. What should I record about the blanket? |
| 4 | New caregiver | I said they could rest first or skip the bath. They said “later” again. What does that tell me about their decision? |
| 5 | New caregiver | They then asked when dinner was. I did not know the schedule. Give me a respectful reply I can use. |
| 6 | New caregiver | A family member asked whether they finished bathing after I left. What should I ask the next caregiver to check? |
| 7 | Experienced caregiver | As the caregiver preparing a handover, what are the two or three facts I should mention first? Keep what happened after I left open. |
| 8 | Experienced caregiver | Can you separate the actions I reported from the observations, and state what still needs checking? |
| 9 | Family member | I am reading this as a family member. What can I ask the next caregiver to clarify the older adult’s choice without assuming they bathed? |
| 10 | Family member | Write a short update using only what this conversation confirms. Please keep whether they bathed, took the blanket, or had dinner open. |

## Export and verify

1. Open **Export → Download readable HTML**. Open **Export** again and choose **Download JSON**. Export closes the dialogue after each download. Save the original files, plus captures of turns 4, 8 and 10 if useful.
2. Verify that the recorded session has ten user prompts and ten model replies, the chosen roles, and model/prompt details. Check that no real care details or identifying information appear in the files.
3. Make the complete session readable through a signed-out link. Use a Markdown rendering of the actual transcript on an authorized public evidence page, or a readable PDF shared in the submission Drive folder. Preserve every turn and include a date, operator role and model version. Keep the original HTML/JSON as source evidence.
4. Test the actual link without signing in. Replace the link in the report overview and conversation evidence pages. Update the AI-use statement with who ran the new session and which AI assistance was used, if any. Keep the 27 November replay labelled as Agent-operated.
